How I run it

Principles

Segment by trust

Personal devices, servers, internet-facing apps, IoT and guests each get their own zone. Lower-trust zones cannot start connections into higher ones.

Least privilege, including for AI

Automation gets exactly the access its job needs, through allow-listed, logged entry points, with full access only in a short break-glass window.

Back up before you touch it

Every guest is backed up nightly and verified weekly. Updates run after backups, and risky changes take a snapshot first with automatic rollback.

Measure, then change

Every tuning decision on this page comes from a benchmark or a packet capture, not a forum post.

Write it down

The lab has a living reference of every service, rule and past incident. It is what both I and the AI agent read before making a change.

Expose as little as possible

Internet-facing apps live in an isolated DMZ, and web apps are published through an outbound-only tunnel rather than open ports.
